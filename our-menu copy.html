Certified & Licensed Services

Licensed & Professionally Certified Event Services

At 4S Catering and Events, we maintain the highest standards of quality, hygiene, safety, and professionalism. Our services operate with all the necessary statutory approvals and industry-recognized certifications, ensuring complete trust and confidence for every event we manage. From food preparation to event execution, every detail is handled with responsibility, care, and professional excellence.

4S Catering and Events is a trusted catering and event management company in Trivandrum, Kerala, offering complete event solutions including wedding catering, corporate catering, stage decoration, photography, videography, makeup services, live counters, accommodation arrangements, and event planning services. We operate with all required statutory licenses including Civic Body License, Food Safety License, FoSTaC Certification, GST Registration, and membership in the All Kerala Caterers Association (AKCA), ensuring quality, hygiene, professionalism, and customer satisfaction for every event.
